FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need internet to use Toolbelt?

No! Toolbelt works completely offline. Create invoices and quotes from the job site, basement, or anywhere with no signal. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

Can I add my own logo to invoices?

Absolutely. Add your business logo, choose your brand colors, and pick from multiple professional templates. Your invoices will look like they came from an established business.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely on your device and protected by iOS security. We don't sell your information or share it with third parties. Your business data stays your business.

How does the AI writing feature work?

Just describe the work in your own words — "fixed leaky pipe, replaced washers" — and our AI transforms it into professional invoice language. You can also use voice input when your hands are busy.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, no contracts or commitments. Cancel anytime directly through your Apple account. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What's included in the free version?

The free version lets you create up to 3 documents per month — an invoice or a quote each counts as one — with all features included. No credit card required, no trial expiration. Upgrade only when you need more.
